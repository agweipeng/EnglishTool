DAILY NEWS DIGEST - 2026-09-09

INTERNATIONAL NEWS
- Ukraine peace push stalled: US envoys Witkoff and Kushner met Putin in Moscow (~Sept 5, 3+ hrs, no breakthrough) then went to Kyiv; Putin said there's "a chance" of a deal but called recent Ukrainian ship/aircraft attacks "state terrorism."
- US-Iran conflict escalated sharply: US struck southern Iran and disabled/destroyed three Iranian oil tankers; Iran retaliated on US bases in Bahrain, Jordan and Iraq and hit Saudi oil facilities, pushing crude toward $100/barrel.
- Nepal's Rasuwa flood catastrophe left ~5,300 missing (incl. ~587 foreign tourists) with 21,000+ personnel mobilized.

US STOCK MARKET
- Tuesday (Sept 8) close, after the Labor Day holiday: Dow -1.18% to 52,786.07, S&P 500 -0.58% to 7,673.52, Nasdaq -0.32% to 26,421.41.
- Surging oil (crude near $100) on Mideast strikes plus caution ahead of a key inflation print drove the selloff; big-cap tech (Apple, Alphabet, Microsoft) lagged.
- Qualcomm jumped ~3% on a major AI-chip deal with Amazon; energy names gained on the oil spike.

BIG TECH
- Apple's Sept 9 event unveiled its first foldable — the book-style iPhone Ultra (5.5"/7.8" OLED, A20 Pro, ~$2,000, most expensive iPhone ever) — plus iPhone 18 Pro/Pro Max on the 2nm A20 Pro, Apple Watch Series 12/Ultra 4, and new AirPods.
- Nvidia confirmed its $12.9B Hugging Face acquisition (open platform, no compute lock-in; closes H1 2027); Jensen Huang topped Glassdoor's 2026 Best CEOs.
- Tesla rose ~4% (Sept 8) with Austin robotaxi rides underway and the Tesla Semi rollout set for late September; Big Tech's combined AI capex is projected near $724B in 2026.

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS
- OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra is the first model to cross its "Critical" cyber threshold (100% on ExploitBench, found two zero-days unsafeguarded); it launched "Daybreak" with $1B in subsidized defensive access for utilities, local govts, and OSS maintainers.
- Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.1/Mythos 5.1 (Sept 1) add a mid-conversation effort dial and a 75% cache-read price cut; Google shipped Gemini 3.8 Flash (with a gated Cyber variant) and Lyria 3.5.
- Meta released Muse Spark 1.3 and Muse Voice Transcribe (80ms real-time chunks); three of four recent frontier launches now ship tiered, access-gated cyber variants.

OPENCLAW
- No brand-new release since 2026.9.2 (reliability/recovery improvements, GPT-6 Astra and Muse Spark 1.3 support, safer candidate-state updates); a Sept 8 item is just a Plugin SDK deprecation, not news.
- No fresh CVE/supply-chain disclosure this window.

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC
- Claude Code v2.1.263 shipped Sept 6 (bug fixes/reliability, follow-on to v2.1.261); the Claude iOS app is gaining a usage-details view and a Claude Code customization section.
- Sept 8: an Anthropic researcher and NYU's Tristan Buckmaster released a Lean-verified proof of finite-time blowup for the 3D Euler equations, produced using Claude.
- Anthropic reportedly walked away from a ~$6B Decart acquisition and is touting ~$517B in compute commitments as IPO investors press for per-token/per-gigawatt metrics ahead of a ~October listing.

CHINA
- August FX reserves rose $19.5B to $3.4383T — the highest since 2015 — aided by a weaker dollar; the PBOC extended gold buying to a 22nd straight month.
- Deflation eased: August PPI improved to ~-3.0% y/y (from -4.4% in July), the narrowest decline in the multi-year streak, while core CPI firmed to +0.9%; exports and the trade surplus stayed strong.
- Xi's expected Washington state visit / Trump summit is reported for ~Sept 24 with a trade-truce extension seen as near-certain; China's SAMR antitrust probe into Nvidia remains a pressure point.

SINGAPORE
- PM Wong announced the first ministerial pay rise in 15 years (up to ~9% one-time): the entry-minister benchmark rises to S$1.8M and the PM's to S$3.6M, with MP allowances up to S$18,500/month from Oct 15.
- The September MAS survey lifted 2026 GDP to ~5% (manufacturing +8.4%, NODX +17%) while trimming headline inflation to 2.1%; manufacturing PMI held at 51.5 (electronics 52.6).
- AI/semiconductor capex keeps surging — Budget 2026's S$800M R&D top-up plus Micron's US$24B megafab and hyperscaler data-center builds.

OUTLOOK: Oil near $100 and Mideast escalation knocked stocks off record highs, but the AI-capex engine (Nvidia-HF, Apple's foldable, Anthropic's IPO run-up) and resilient China/Singapore data keep the growth trade intact — geopolitics the swing factor.
