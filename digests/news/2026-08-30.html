DAILY NEWS DIGEST - 2026-08-30

INTERNATIONAL NEWS
- Trump announced a sweeping US-Venezuela oil deal giving a new US-controlled company 55% of 17 fields (65B+ barrels) under 100-year rights; Caracas expects ~$209B, following January's raid that captured Maduro.

US STOCK MARKET

BIG TECH

- Apple set a Sept 9 event expected to unveil the iPhone 18 Pro/Pro Max and a foldable "iPhone Ultra" on a 2nm A20 Pro chip; no standard iPhone 18 this cycle.
- Tesla's Robotaxi expanded to 6am-10pm daily across all cities with the Cybercab slated for Sept 3; Amazon will shut Mechanical Turk on Sept 30 and tripled its Nvidia chip order.

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS

- Model cadence stayed hot: Z.AI's GLM-5.3 Flash (Aug 26) plus 14 August releases; Google's A2A protocol joined the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation (250+ members).

OPENCLAW
- New beta v2026.8.1-beta.3 (Aug 24): GPT-5.6 reasoning across OpenClaw/Codex runtime, verified SQLite backup/restore, Puppeteer-compatible CDP relay.
- Security is the dominant storyline — researchers keep detailing chained attack paths; the SecureClaw open-source tool debuted for runtime hardening.
- Now ~388k GitHub stars; runs as a non-profit foundation backed by OpenAI, NVIDIA, Microsoft.

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC
- A US judge ruled (Aug 28) the Pentagon's "supply chain risk" blacklisting of Anthropic illegal retaliation for refusing military surveillance/autonomous-weapons use.
- Salesforce + Anthropic unveiled "Claudeforce" (Aug 28), embedding Claude as default reasoning across Agentforce/Slack with 37 prebuilt sales skills; Claude Desktop added a native browser side panel.
- Anthropic's ~$45B, six-year Nscale compute deal (~460 MW, Nvidia Vera Rubin) is confirmed; Files API and Agent Skills API reached GA.

CHINA

- Property crisis persists: state-linked Vanke reported a ~$2.2B loss (Aug 27) atop a record ~$13B annual loss.
- ByteDance and Tencent each received ~10,000 Nvidia H200s as Beijing eases curbs; XPeng's robotics unit raised $900M+ (Tencent, Alibaba).

SINGAPORE

- DBS posted a record Q2 (1H net profit +5% to S$6.01B) and raised guidance; Grab lifted 2026 revenue guidance to $4.10-4.15B with a $750M buyback.
- National Day Rally follow-through: HDB income ceiling to rise so most couples keep access to subsidized housing.

OUTLOOK: A hawkish Warsh Fed capped the AI rally into the weekend — AI capex (Nvidia-HF, Anthropic compute) still drives the tape, but rate risk and China's slowdown are the counterweights.
