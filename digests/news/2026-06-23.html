DAILY NEWS DIGEST - 2026-06-23

INTERNATIONAL NEWS
- US-Iran talks in Switzerland produced a 60-day roadmap and a draft oil-sanctions exemption; Qatar and Pakistan cite "encouraging progress" despite Trump's continued hardline rhetoric.
- UK PM Keir Starmer resigned June 22, triggering a leadership transition in Britain.
- US-China tensions escalated: Pentagon blacklisted Alibaba, Baidu, and BYD; Beijing retaliated with sanctions on 10 US defense companies and restricted rare earth exports.

US STOCK MARKET
- S&P 500 fell ~1.2%, Dow shed 500+ points after the Fed held rates at 3.5–3.75% but signaled a hawkish shift — 9 of 18 policymakers now project at least one hike by year-end.
- Markets logged their worst "Fed day" reaction under current leadership since 1994; chip stocks cushioned declines earlier in the week but could not offset the macro headwind.

BIG TECH
- Alphabet announced an $80B equity raise dedicated to AI compute infrastructure buildout.
- Microsoft launched Scout, an always-on AI personal agent integrated into M365, Teams, and Outlook; Nvidia expanded its Windows + Azure partnership with Microsoft for agentic AI deployment.
- SpaceX completed a record $75B IPO; Tesla continues to face market share pressure as BYD holds the top global EV position.

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS
- Google Gemini 3.5 Flash is now the default model in Google Search's AI Mode globally; Qwen3 Coder Next and Inception Mercury 2 — a diffusion-based reasoning model at 1,000+ tokens/sec — also launched this week.
- Foxconn's MoMClaw multi-agent manufacturing system cut root-cause analysis time 80% and machine failures 10%; AI inference costs continue falling up to 100× as 320+ models released in June alone.

OPENCLAW
- v2026.6.9 pre-release ships richer Telegram HTML rendering, standalone npm provider plugins, iOS Watch controls, and a full agent recovery overhaul; project now at 310,000+ GitHub stars.

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC
- Claude Code added agent teams, nested skills, improved permissions, and a /goal command for context persistence across turns; Claude Design hit 1M+ users in its first week.
- Claude Corps opened a $150M fellowship funding 1,000 AI workers at nonprofits; a 90-minute global outage hit flagship Claude models on June 22 before full restoration.

CHINA
- New outbound investment rules effective July 1 require AI startups to list domestically or in Hong Kong, blocking cross-border M&A (e.g., Manus AI's planned sale to Meta).
- 15th Five-Year Plan accelerates AI, robotics, quantum, biomanufacturing, and 6G; Q1 2026 GDP grew 5% but full-year growth expected to moderate to ~4.5%.

SINGAPORE
- Digital economy reached S$128.1B (18.6% of GDP), with semiconductor and AI data center demand as key growth drivers.
- MAS raised the S$NEER appreciation slope to 1% in April and revised core inflation upward to 1.5–2.5%; 2026 GDP growth projected at 2–4%.

OUTLOOK: Fed's hawkish surprise dominates near-term market sentiment; US-China blacklist escalation and UK political transition add geopolitical uncertainty heading into the week.
