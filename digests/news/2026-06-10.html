DAILY NEWS DIGEST - June 10, 2026

INTERNATIONAL NEWS
- Philippines struck by magnitude 7.8 offshore earthquake, killing 32+, injuring 200+, and sending a 3-foot tsunami into nearby coasts.

- DRC Ebola outbreak spreading at an "unprecedented pace," Africa CDC warns.
- Pope Leo XIV addressed the Spanish legislature, calling for "moral renewal" in public life.

US STOCK MARKET
- S&P 500 fell ~1%, Nasdaq 100 dropped ~2% on June 9 as investors rotated out of tech; Nasdaq had its worst single day since April 2025 tariff turmoil on June 4 (-4%).
- Nvidia, Oracle, AMD each fell 1-3%; Apple lost 3% after EU antitrust rules blocked its new Siri AI from launching in Europe.
- Tom Lee issued a rare bearish warning: says market "feels like a bear market correction" coming later in 2026; AI chip/datacenter rally seen as potentially overextended.

BIG TECH
- Apple WWDC 2026 (June 8): Siri AI debuts as a conversational assistant built with Google — on-screen context awareness, image editing, writing tools, dedicated app with history.
- Meta disclosed 20,000+ Instagram accounts hijacked after attackers abused its AI-powered support system to reset passwords.
- Industry coins "MANGO" (Meta, Anthropic, Nvidia, Google, OpenAI) as the dominant AI-era group; Nvidia leads all companies at $5.2T market cap.

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS
- OpenAI launched GPT-5.5 (and Pro/Instant variants) plus the OpenAI Deployment Company — partnerships with Bain, McKinsey, Capgemini to deploy AI inside large enterprises.
- Anthropic CEO disclosed Claude hit a $30B annualized revenue run rate in Q1 2026 — described as 80x growth in a single quarter.
- Microsoft released MAI-Thinking-1 (1T-param reasoning model) and MAI-Code-1-Flash (137B, GitHub Copilot-optimized). Google's Gemini 3.5 Flash outperforms Gemini 3.1 Pro at 4x speed.
- New releases this week: NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B, Alibaba Qwen3 Coder Next, MiniMax M2.7.

OPENCLAW
- v2026.6.5 shipped: parallel web search support, stronger MCP/Anthropic recovery, improved Google Vertex/Matrix handling, safer plugin installs via operator policy.
- Windows companion app now available; OpenClaw node and gateway run securely on Windows via MXC.

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC
- Claude Code launched dynamic workflows (research preview): spawns tens to hundreds of parallel subagents per session with built-in verification, across CLI, Desktop, VS Code, API, and cloud platforms.
- New "ultracode" effort setting enables xhigh reasoning and auto-selects workflows for complex tasks.
- Billing change effective June 15: agent SDK, claude -p, GitHub Actions, and third-party agents move off subscription limits onto separate monthly credits ($20/$100/$200 tiers), metered at full API rates, no rollover.

CHINA
- State Council issued new Overseas Investment Regulations (Decree 837, effective July 1): bans exporting restricted tech, data, or knowhow under the guise of outbound investment.
- Xi-Putin summit reaffirmed deep strategic cooperation; BeiDou–GLONASS satellite navigation link-up set under 2026–2030 roadmap.
- 15th Five-Year Plan targets R&D growth ≥7%/year and digital economy at 12.5% of GDP by 2030; GDP growth target 4.5–5.0%.

SINGAPORE
- National AI Council launched, chaired by PM Lawrence Wong; 60+ AI Centres of Excellence now operating, including Prudential, Grab, GlobalFoundries.
- Singapore mandates Sustainable Aviation Fuel blending in 2026 — first in Asia; carbon tax rises to S$45/tonne, highest in Asia.
- Economy on track for resilient growth, underpinned by AI boom and semiconductor expansion under the S$37B RIE 2030 masterplan.

OUTLOOK: AI fundamentals remain strong (Claude $30B run rate, GPT-5.5, WWDC AI overhaul) but markets are rotating out of tech on valuation concerns — June 15 Anthropic billing change is a key signal of enterprise AI monetization maturing.
