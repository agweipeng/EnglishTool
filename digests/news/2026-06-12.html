DAILY NEWS DIGEST - June 12, 2026

INTERNATIONAL NEWS

- Pope Leo XIV traveled to Spain's Canary Islands — a major European migrant entry point — days after criticizing European immigration policy before Spain's Parliament.

US STOCK MARKET
- June 11 close (most recent): S&P 500 +1.8%, Nasdaq +2.5%, Dow +923 pts to 50,841 — driven by oil price pullback after Trump backed off threatened Iran strikes.
- Chip stocks led: Micron +11%, AMD +8%, Lam Research +12.7%, Intel +10%+ — SpaceX IPO listing today (June 12) is being watched as the next major market sentiment event.

BIG TECH
- Nvidia retains the world's largest market cap heading into mid-June 2026; Magnificent 7 profits projected to grow ~18% for the full year.
- Tesla pivoted strategy in early 2026 to discontinue Model S and X, concentrating resources on the Optimus humanoid robot program.
- Combined 2026 AI capex from Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, and Meta projected at $649B, up from $411B in 2025.

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS
- Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5 (Mythos-class), achieving 95% on SWE-bench Verified; priced at $10/$50 per million input/output tokens.
- Nvidia released Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B, a hybrid architecture alternating attention layers with Mamba-2 state-space layers for improved long-context efficiency.
- Open-weight models from Llama, Qwen, and DeepSeek are now matching proprietary benchmarks on multiple tasks, compressing the gap with closed frontier models.

OPENCLAW
- v2026.6.5 (June 9) fixes MCP tool-result coercion issues, improves chat safety (QQBot strips reasoning tags before sending), and restores extended-thinking sessions after prompt-cache expiry.
- Microsoft's Scout assistant — built on the OpenClaw framework — is being positioned as an always-on agent for Microsoft 365 workflows.

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC
- Claude Code v2.1.170: Claude Fable 5 access, post-session hook, /cd command for changing working directories, and tightened MCP policy enforcement.
- New "Workflows" feature lets Claude write orchestration scripts that execute across multiple background subagents when tasks exceed single-conversation scope.

CHINA
- China's 15th Five-Year Plan is driving targeted investment in AI, robotics, quantum, and 6G; new regulations effective July 1 require promising tech startups to list domestically or in Hong Kong.
- US-China trade is in managed competition mode — some tariffs rolled back, new export controls paused, military hotlines reopened — though structural rivalry in AI and semiconductors persists.

SINGAPORE
- Singapore's Budget 2026 enhanced the Enterprise Innovation Scheme with a 400% tax deduction on qualifying AI expenditures (up to S$50K/year), reinforcing its AI economy push.
- 58% of Singapore employers plan to freeze headcount in 2026 and 18% are already eliminating roles due to AI; the government is responding with upskilling incentives and new growth sectors.

OUTLOOK: Iran conflict inflation and SpaceX IPO demand are the twin June 12 market themes; Claude Fable 5 and Nemotron 3 Ultra mark another week of frontier model escalation.
