DAILY NEWS DIGEST - 2026-06-01

INTERNATIONAL NEWS
- US-Iran war escalated over the weekend: US struck Iranian radar and drone-control sites at Goruk and Qeshm Island; Iran's IRGC retaliated against a US air base; Kuwait intercepted Iranian missiles. Ceasefire talks remain stalled as Trump told Americans to "sit back and relax."
- France seized sanctioned Russian oil tanker Tagor in the Atlantic with UK support—the latest EU action against Russia's shadow fleet. Israel deepened its Lebanon ground offensive, troops seizing the Beaufort fortress in the deepest incursion in 26 years.
- WHO chief flew to DR Congo as Ebola killed 40+ across Congo and Uganda; Brazil is investigating possible imported cases.

US STOCK MARKET
- US equities finished May on a nine-week winning streak: S&P 500 ~7,580 (record high), NASDAQ Composite +8.4% for the month, Dow near 49,500—the longest S&P rally since December 2023.
- Key movers: Dell and Snowflake surged 30%+ on earnings; Micron hit $1T market cap alongside SK Hynix; gold near $4,500/oz, silver above $81/oz.
- Investors eye the June 16-17 FOMC meeting, PCE inflation data due this week, and potential Supreme Court tariff rulings.

BIG TECH
- NVIDIA unveiled RTX Spark at Computex Taipei: an ARM-based N1X superchip for PCs from Dell, HP, Microsoft Surface, ASUS, Lenovo and MSI—arriving fall 2026. Jensen Huang confirmed Vera data-center CPU is in full production; Anthropic, OpenAI and SpaceX are among first customers.
- US closed a Blackwell-chip export loophole, now requiring a licence to ship top AI chips to subsidiaries of Chinese companies based outside China.

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS
- Anthropic shipped Claude Opus 4.8 (May 28): benchmark improvements, dynamic workflows (hundreds of parallel subagents per session), effort control on claude.ai, and 3× cheaper fast mode. Project Glasswing update: Mythos Preview found 23,019 vulnerabilities in 1,000+ open-source projects; broader Mythos release expected "in coming weeks."
- NVIDIA launched Nemotron 3 Ultra (550B params, 300+ tok/s) at Computex—top-ranked US open-weights model—plus Alpamayo 2 Super (32B reasoning VLA for robotaxis). Google's Gemini 3.5 Pro is announced for June with no fixed date.

OPENCLAW
- v2026.5.31-beta.4 released June 1: agents and CLI recover more cleanly from interrupted tool calls, stale session bindings, and compaction handoffs; Tailscale Serve binding and Communication notification settings added.
- v2026.5.26 stable (May 27): transcript-backed meeting summaries, Gateway metadata caching for major speed gains, and six security patches including SSRF and prompt-injection fixes. Project now at 376K GitHub stars.

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC
- Dynamic workflows launched in research preview for Claude Code (Max/Team/Enterprise): Claude writes orchestration scripts that spin up hundreds of parallel subagents, enabling codebase-scale migrations across 100k+ lines in a single session.
- Anthropic confirmed as an early Vera CPU customer (per NVIDIA Computex keynote). Mythos-class models expected to reach all customers once safeguards clear—Anthropic says that is "coming weeks" away.

CHINA
- China's official manufacturing PMI slipped to 50.0 in May (from 50.3)—a 3-month low—as new orders fell into contraction (49.9). The private RatingDog PMI held at 51.8, above forecasts. High-tech manufacturing remains bright at 52.9, extending 16 months in expansion.
- Beijing issued sweeping new overseas-deal rules effective July 1, banning cross-border talent transfers in sensitive sectors without approval—a direct follow-up to blocking Meta's Manus acquisition. The rules explicitly target "Singapore-washing" and give Beijing power to unwind completed transactions.

SINGAPORE
- MTI held its 2026 GDP forecast at 2–4% but flagged significantly higher downside risks from the Iran war; Q1 GDP was revised up to 6% YoY, a six-quarter high. Enterprise Singapore upgraded NODX export forecast to 3–5% on a 57.8% Q1 electronics surge driven by AI demand.
- National AI Strategy refreshed at ATxSummit: OpenAI committed S$300M and its first Applied AI Lab outside the US to Singapore. China's new overseas-deal rules—targeting "Singapore-washing"—raise fresh compliance risks for Chinese-linked startups registered here.

OUTLOOK: Nine weeks of US equity gains face a geopolitical stress test—Iran-war escalation and Hormuz shipping risk are now the dominant macro variable, with FOMC and PCE data adding near-term uncertainty.
