DAILY NEWS DIGEST - 2026-05-25 (Monday)

INTERNATIONAL NEWS
- Trump says a US-Iran deal is "largely negotiated" and could be signed within days, centered on a 60-day ceasefire extension and reopening the Strait of Hormuz; Iran has not confirmed and Israel is reportedly unhappy (NPR, Times of Israel).
- Secretary of State Marco Rubio arrived in India for a Quad foreign ministers meeting (US, India, Australia, Japan) on Indo-Pacific strategy (NPR).
- Russia-Ukraine talks remain stalled after the May 9-11 ceasefire and a 1,000-for-1,000 prisoner swap; Putin signaled openness to direct talks with Zelenskyy (Al Jazeera).

US STOCK MARKET (Friday May 22 close; US markets closed Mon for Memorial Day)
- Dow rose ~294 pts (+0.74%) to a record ~50,580; S&P 500 +0.4% to ~7,473; Nasdaq +0.2% to ~26,344 (CNBC, TheStreet).
- S&P 500 extended its winning streak to eight straight weeks, led by tech and industrials.
- Dell surged ~16% on analyst upgrades ahead of Q1 FY27 earnings due May 28.

BIG TECH
- Nvidia beat earnings but the stock fell May 22; it has committed ~$90B backing 145+ AI firms over 16 months (Yahoo Finance).
- Meta began cutting ~8,000 roles (10%) amid heavy AI capex; Alphabet raised 2026 capex guidance to $180-190B (TechStartups, Fast Company).
- Microsoft signed a $9.7B compute deal with IREN; Google and Blackstone are backing a new AI cloud venture to expand TPU demand.

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS
- Google rolled out Gemini 3.5 Flash (May 19) plus "Gemini Spark," a personal AI agent in beta, and a new world model "Omni" (CNBC).
- No new frontier model dropped over the weekend; GPT-5.5 Instant and xAI Grok 4.3 remain the latest major releases (early May).
- Anthropic reported $10.9B revenue and its first profit; OpenAI has filed for an IPO (BuildFastWithAI).

OPENCLAW
- OpenClaw shipped stable release 2026.5.18 with Control UI improvements and a faster Mac app (GitHub releases).
- NVIDIA announced "NemoClaw" for the OpenClaw community, collaborating on model isolation and local data-access security (NVIDIA Newsroom).

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC
- At Code with Claude, Anthropic expanded its platform with higher API limits and Managed Agents features (multiagent orchestration, webhooks) (Anthropic, InfoQ).
- KPMG signed a global alliance, giving 276,000+ employees Claude access (KPMG).
- Claude Security entered public beta for Enterprise (codebase vuln scanning); Anthropic says Opus 4.7 has patched 2,100+ vulnerabilities in three weeks.

CHINA
- Q1 2026 GDP grew 5.0%, but April retail sales rose just 0.2% y/y (weakest since Dec 2022) and home prices fell sharply; 2026 growth target set at 4.5-5% (SCMP, CNBC).
- PBOC held LPRs steady for a 12th month (1Y 3.0%, 5Y 3.5%); offshore yuan near 6.80 (Trading Economics).
- ByteDance, Tencent and Alibaba are scrambling for Huawei AI chips after DeepSeek's V4 launch (Reuters).

SINGAPORE
- Q1 2026 GDP grew 4.6% y/y; MTI upgraded its 2026 forecast to 2.0-4.0% on AI tailwinds (SingStat/MTI).
- DBS Q1 net profit rose ~1% to a record S$2.93B on record wealth fees and 17% ROE (Reuters).
- Micron's US$24B NAND fab expansion (~1,600 jobs) anchors Singapore's RIE 2030 chip push (DigiTimes).

OUTLOOK: Risk-on mood holds as US indices sit at records and AI capex surges, but soft China demand and a fragile Iran deal are the key swing factors this week.
