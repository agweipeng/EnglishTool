DAILY NEWS DIGEST - 2026-05-28

INTERNATIONAL NEWS

BIG TECH

- Alphabet is 2026's top Mag-7 performer (+23% YTD); Meta fell 8%+ despite strong earnings as investors balked at rising AI spend with unclear payoff timelines.

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS
- Gemini 3.5 Flash reached general availability: frontier-level intelligence at 4x the speed of comparable models, priced at $1.50/$9 per 1M tokens with 1M context window.
- GPT-5.5 Instant became the new default ChatGPT model; SubQ raised $29M in seed for a non-transformer architecture claiming ~1/5 frontier model cost on long-context workloads.

- Claude Mythos solved a 56-year-old open mathematical conjecture; Google AlphaProof solved two hard problems in a single day — AI mathematical reasoning reaching new milestones.

OPENCLAW
- OpenClaw shipped v2026.5.26-beta.2 with faster replies, richer voice/Talk controls, safer agent behavior, and deeper observability. Project now has 350,000+ GitHub stars.
- After a rough patch in late April (gateway slowdowns, plugin dependency loops), a comprehensive security roadmap published May 15 introduces filesystem safeguards, network egress control, and plugin integrity checks.

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC
- Anthropic raised Claude Code weekly usage limits by 50% through July 13, 2026 for Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans — a direct competitive move against OpenAI's Codex.
- Managed Agents can now run in enterprise-controlled sandboxes with access to private MCP servers; /usage command shows per-category limit breakdowns for skills, subagents, and plugins.
- The "Code w/ Claude" event in London brought together builders and developers for two days of keynotes and workshops with Anthropic's engineering teams.

CHINA
- China posted Q1 2026 GDP growth of 5.0%, though analysts flag weak consumption, data inconsistencies, and Iran war-driven inflation as headwinds.
- Semiconductor imports hit a record $135B last quarter, up 27% YoY, as domestic AI compute demand skyrockets despite U.S. export controls.
- Huawei revealed a roadmap for chips equivalent to a 1.4nm process node within five years; China's NDRC blocked Meta's acquisition of Manus, the AI agent startup with Chinese roots.

SINGAPORE
- Singapore's Q1 2026 GDP grew 6.0% YoY, beating the advance estimate of 4.6%, driven by wholesale trade, manufacturing, and finance — powered by AI-related demand.
- Multiple banks have upgraded full-year forecasts: Maybank now sees 4.2% growth (up from 3.4%); UOB raised its forecast to 3.2%. MTI maintains its official 2%–4% range.
- Budget 2026 targets AI transformation across four key sectors and commits S$37B to research, innovation, and enterprise; downside risk remains from the Iran conflict.

OUTLOOK: AI capex commitments and strong tech earnings continue to drive equity markets to records, but geopolitical risk from the Iran conflict remains the key variable to watch.
