DAILY NEWS DIGEST - June 11, 2026

INTERNATIONAL NEWS
- US-Iran military conflict escalated sharply: the US struck Iran for a second consecutive day; Iran retaliated with missiles targeting the US Fifth Fleet in Bahrain and bases in Kuwait and Jordan, and declared the Strait of Hormuz fully closed.
- A US military strike on a tanker off Oman killed three Indian sailors, creating diplomatic friction ahead of a Modi-Trump bilateral meeting at the G7.
- Violence spread across Belfast after a knife attack allegedly by a Sudanese asylum seeker triggered anti-immigration riots across Northern Ireland.
- Amnesty International accused Israel of a state-directed campaign of "ethnic cleansing" in the occupied West Bank, citing government policy as a driver of forced displacement.

US STOCK MARKET
- Markets closed sharply higher: S&P 500 +1.8%, Nasdaq +2.5%, Dow +930 pts to ~50,841 — driven by Trump pulling back on further Iran strikes and a resulting decline in oil prices.
- Chip stocks led: Lam Research +12.7%, Micron +11%, Intel +10% (Bank of America upgrade on CPU demand), AMD +8%; SpaceX IPO positioning ahead of June 12 listing also boosted sentiment broadly.

BIG TECH
- Intel surged ~10% after a Bank of America upgrade citing soaring CPU orders — standout tech mover of the day.
- Nvidia gained ~2.2% on recovering chip sentiment; Arm Holdings fell after its CEO warned smartphone unit growth is set to turn negative due to a memory chip shortage.
- Combined 2026 AI capex from Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, and Meta is projected at $649B, up from $411B in 2025.

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS
- GPT-5.5 Pro leads research leaderboards; Claude Mythos Preview and Claude Opus 4.8 follow closely; NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B among notable recent open-weight releases.
- New frontier models are arriving roughly every 2 days; multimodal capabilities are now standard across leading models, with GPT-4-class performance available at significantly lower cost.

OPENCLAW
- v2026.6.5 (released June 9) added parallel web search as a built-in provider, stricter chat safety, and fixes for MCP tools erroring on complex image/audio results.
- Microsoft announced Scout, an always-on agentic assistant built on the OpenClaw framework, aimed at integrating OpenClaw-style agent capabilities into Microsoft 365.

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC
- Claude Code v2.1.170 ships Claude Fable 5 access, a post-session hook, /cd command for moving working directories, and tightened MCP policy enforcement.
- Sub-agents can now spawn their own sub-agents up to 5 levels deep; Amazon Bedrock integration gains automatic AWS region detection from ~/.aws config files.

CHINA
- China issued sweeping new regulations tightening controls over overseas technology transfers and granting explicit authority to retaliate against governments that restrict Chinese investments.
- China's tech stocks hit bear-market territory on June 11; the OECD reported Chinese firms receive 3–8x more government subsidies than OECD peers, a finding Beijing's commerce ministry rejected as "one-sided."

SINGAPORE
- Singapore raised its 2026 GDP growth forecast, citing global economic momentum and surging AI infrastructure demand underpinned by the S$37B RIE 2030 masterplan.
- Singapore became the first Asian nation to mandate Sustainable Aviation Fuel blending in 2026; carbon tax rose to S$45/tonne, the highest in Asia.

OUTLOOK: Markets rebounding on Iran de-escalation signals, but PPI surge and Strait of Hormuz closure risk keep macro uncertainty elevated — SpaceX IPO on June 12 is the key near-term sentiment gauge.
