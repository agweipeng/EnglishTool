DAILY NEWS DIGEST - 2026-08-25

INTERNATIONAL NEWS
- Russia launched a large-scale missile and drone strike on Kyiv; Ukraine has publicly stated it has "zero interceptors" remaining and is urgently seeking Patriot resupply before winter, when Russia historically targets energy infrastructure.
- The US imposed 50% tariffs on $20B of Canadian products; Canada had suspended trade talks last week citing insufficient progress.
- Iran and Oman are negotiating an agreement requiring maritime fee payments to a new corporation for Strait of Hormuz passage — a significant departure from established freedom of navigation norms.

US STOCK MARKET
- Monday Aug 24 close: S&P 500 -0.28% to 7,652; Nasdaq -0.77% to 25,980; Dow +0.26%. Chips led the decline: Micron -5.8%, AMD -3%, Nvidia -2.91%, Tesla -3.83%.
- The 30-year Treasury yield hit a 19-year high above 5.3%, pressuring rate-sensitive equities.
- This week's two pivots: Nvidia Q2 FY27 earnings Wednesday (consensus ~$91B, +95% YoY) and Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's inaugural Jackson Hole keynote Friday.

BIG TECH
- Amazon disclosed its AI and semiconductor businesses have each surpassed $25B annualized run rate, both growing at triple-digit rates.
- Tesla is recalling ~2.98 million vehicles (Model 3/Y/S/X) in China over emergency door-release handles starting September 25 — a record recall in China.
- Nvidia trades ~$215-$218 ahead of Wednesday earnings; Microsoft posted the largest single-day market cap appreciation on record following its most recent quarterly report.

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS
- EU AI Act Article 50 transparency obligations took effect August 2, requiring disclosure of AI interactions and machine-readable watermarks on AI-generated content across the EU.
- Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 scores 95% on SWE-bench Verified — 11 points ahead of the next-best model; Fable 5.1 is anticipated.
- MCP (Model Context Protocol) shipped a major release candidate as the emerging standard for agent-to-enterprise-system connections.

OPENCLAW
- August update adds Claude Code temporary access via `openclaw attach`; also upgraded Telegram/Slack/Discord threading, attachments, and voice sessions.
- Platform at 135,000+ GitHub stars; Reco.ai flagged OpenClaw's broad shell/file/browser access as an enterprise security concern requiring governance policies.

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC
- August fixes: auto-mode failures when attribution headers are disabled, MCP OAuth with strict authorization servers, Remote Control stuck spinners; context cost of the built-in claude-api skill cut from ~200K to ~25K tokens.
- Claude Fable 5's 95% SWE-bench score is the benchmark powering expanded Claude Code agentic coding capabilities.

CHINA
- Alibaba issued $10.2B in new Hong Kong shares for its global AI push; shares fell ~10% on dilution.
- China paused rare-earth export controls for another year (near-term relief for US manufacturers); US-China tariffs stabilized at 31%; Standard Chartered raised China 2026 GDP forecast to 4.6%.

SINGAPORE
- MAS launched a Hedge Fund Investment Program and a new ONE Pass Investment Management Track to attract global investment talent; Singapore's AUM has reached nearly S$7 trillion (+7.5%/yr over 5 years).
- Singapore's National AI Council, chaired by the Prime Minister, is now formally established to direct the national AI agenda.

OUTLOOK: Nvidia earnings and Jackson Hole this week are dual inflection points that could simultaneously reprice AI infrastructure expectations and the rate trajectory — the most important two-day sequence since the March tech selloff.
