DAILY NEWS DIGEST - 2026-09-05

INTERNATIONAL NEWS
- US-Iran conflict re-escalated: US forces struck IRGC targets from Sept 1 after attempted attacks on Strait of Hormuz shipping and US personnel, ending a month-long lull; oil rose and Tehran retaliated against Gulf neighbors.
- Putin said there's a chance of a deal to end the Ukraine war, claiming US and China backing; a Ukrainian strike on Belgorod killed four as the daily drone war intensified.
- Xi attended the SCO summit in Bishkek (Sept 1), pledging to "step up peace efforts," and is lining up state visits ahead of an expected late-September US trip to meet Trump.

US STOCK MARKET
- Friday (Sept 4) close: Dow -0.51% to 53,414.25, S&P 500 -0.38% to 7,718.60, Nasdaq -0.29% to 26,506.99 — dragged by a hot jobs report.
- August payrolls surged +162k (vs ~53k expected), unemployment steady at 4.1%; the upside pushed the 2-year yield to its highest since Jan 2025 and lifted September rate-hike odds to ~59%.
- Movers: Tesla fell >6% after an underwhelming Cybercab launch; VW jumped 6.1% on a 50k job-cut plan; Snowflake beat ($0.62 EPS vs $0.45).

BIG TECH
- Nvidia signed a definitive ~$13B deal to acquire Hugging Face (~$11.9B to holders + ~$1B retention), adding a platform with 18M+ developers and 3M+ models; expected to close H1 2027.
- Tesla launched its steering-wheel-free Cybercab robotaxi in Austin (Sept 3); NHTSA opened a probe a day later over how it was certified without a brake pedal or mirrors.
- Google won an ad-tech antitrust ruling (a judge rejected forcing an ad-exchange divestiture); Apple's iPhone event is Sept 9 — the first under new CEO John Ternus, with a folding phone and "Siri AI" expected.

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS
- OpenAI launched GPT-6 Astra (Sept 3), its most powerful model, headlined by "computer use" and rated its first "Critical" cyber model; priced $10/M input, enterprise-first rollout. Brockman called it "the start of AGI."
- Google shipped Gemini 3.8 Flash (its third Flash in six weeks) with a new government/enterprise cybersecurity model; Microsoft's MAI-Transcribe-2 topped the FLEURS benchmark (5.2% WER across 60 languages).
- Meta's Superintelligence Labs released Muse Voice Transcribe, continuing its pivot from open Llama to proprietary models.

OPENCLAW
- OpenClaw 2.0 shipped (early Sept) with simplified setup and collaborative agents; version 2026.9.1 (Sept 3) added Mermaid rendering in the Control UI and mobile apps, an `npx openclaw@latest` quick-start that auto-detects Claude Code/Codex logins, and personal skill libraries on shared Gateways.
- The founder-joins-OpenAI / non-profit-foundation story remains February-2026 background, not new.

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC
- Anthropic shipped Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 (Sept 1) for demanding reasoning, long-running agents, and document-heavy work, plus Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (monitoring data kept in the customer's own cloud) and a 75% cache-read price cut.
- Claude Sonnet 5's promo pricing ended Aug 31 — standard $3/$15 per M took effect Sept 1; Claude Code baseline usage limits rise 25% from Sept 14.
- The Sept 1 triple release is framed as an "IPO playbook" toward a targeted ~$2T listing; Anthropic has reportedly raised ~$130B cumulatively.

CHINA
- August Caixin manufacturing PMI rose to 51.5 (from 50.9), the highest since Feb 2024, with new orders up a 15th straight month — even as deflation persists into a ~10th quarter.
- Xi used the SCO summit to build diplomatic clout ahead of a late-September Trump meeting; the reciprocal-tariff suspension runs to Nov 10 and China is committed to ~$17B/yr of US farm buys.
- Chips: Nvidia restarted H200 production for China (≤75k units/customer for ~10 cleared firms); Huawei's new AI accelerator passed customer testing and SMIC reached volume 5nm (DUV).

SINGAPORE
- MAS's September forecaster survey lifted 2026 GDP to 5.0% (from 3.5%), with manufacturing at 8.4% and NODX 17.0%, on surging AI exports; Q2 GDP grew 5.9%.
- MAS released a stablecoin consultation (P015-2026, Sept 1) formalizing a single-currency stablecoin regime with fully-backed reserves and freeze/burn powers; consultation closes Oct 16.
- PM Wong attended the Singapore-Thailand Leaders' Retreat in Bangkok (Sept 2); ~29% of forecasters flag an AI-bubble burst as a key risk.

OUTLOOK: A hot jobs print revived rate-hike fears just as GPT-6 Astra and the Nvidia-Hugging Face deal reaffirm the AI capex boom — momentum intact, but rate and Iran risk are the near-term brakes.
