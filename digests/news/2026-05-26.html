DAILY NEWS DIGEST - 2026-05-26 (Tuesday)

INTERNATIONAL NEWS
- Iran declared a "controlled maritime zone" in the Strait of Hormuz, demanding transit authorization (reportedly tolls up to $2M, payable in yuan or bitcoin) as the US tightens pressure on Iranian ports; ~20% of global oil and gas passes through the strait (WEF, Al Jazeera).
- After the May 14-15 Trump-Xi summit in Beijing, Trump said Xi offered to help broker peace with Iran and keep Hormuz open; both leaders agreed Iran cannot have a nuclear weapon (Time, CNN).
- Xi reportedly told Trump China will not arm Iran but will keep buying Iranian oil, while signaling interest in buying more US oil (CNBC, Al Jazeera).

US STOCK MARKET
- US markets reopen Tuesday after Monday's Memorial Day close; analysts flag elevated volatility in the first 60-90 minutes (Benzinga).
- Last session (Fri May 22) ended at records: Dow +294 pts, S&P 500 +0.37% to 7,473, Nasdaq +0.19% to 26,344 (TheStreet).
- For the week, Dow +2.13%, S&P 500 +0.88%, Nasdaq +0.45%, helped by Middle East peace optimism (Investtech).

BIG TECH
- Nvidia is the world's largest public company (~$5.2T) after record Q1 FY27 revenue of $81.6B (+85% y/y), $75.2B data-center; guided Q2 to ~$91B with an added $80B buyback (SEC filings).
- Tesla pressured after flagging 2026 capex above $25B and negative free cash flow for the rest of the year; will soon allow Apple Watch as a car key (Yahoo Finance).
- Meta began its 10% workforce cut (~8,000 roles); Microsoft signed a $9.7B compute deal with IREN; Google and Blackstone are backing a new AI cloud venture (CNBC).

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS
- Google released Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite, ~2.5x faster responses at $0.25 per million input tokens; Gemini Spark agent rolling out in beta (CNBC).
- An OpenAI model reportedly disproved a central conjecture in discrete geometry (May 22), a notable AI-for-math result (LLM-Stats).
- OpenAI added C2PA provenance, SynthID watermarking, and a public image-verification tool (OpenAI News).

OPENCLAW
- OpenClaw shipped v2026.5.25-alpha.1: faster gateway auth, better onboarding, chat/session UI polish, stronger plugin/SDK support, plus fixes across Telegram/Discord/Windows/macOS (Releasebot).
- Recent builds added a file-transfer plugin (16MB/round-trip ceiling) and Docker security hardening (NVIDIA's NemoClaw enterprise layer announced at GTC 2026) (Blink Blog).

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC
- Anthropic raised Claude Code and Opus API limits and added Managed Agents features (dreaming, multiagent orchestration, webhooks) (InfoWorld).
- Claude Code shipped pinned background sessions, a renamed /code-review flow, remote desktop-to-phone control, and a redesigned split-view GUI (changelog).
- Added Compliance API integrations and self-hosted sandbox support; some users reported brief service disruptions May 25 (Anthropic News).

CHINA
- PBOC held LPRs steady for a 12th month (1Y 3.0%, 5Y 3.5%); offshore yuan strengthened to ~6.78, its strongest since Feb 2023, on a weaker dollar (Trading Economics, SCMP).
- Huawei unveiled a "LogicFolding" chip-design breakthrough for Kirin phones now and Ascend AI chips by 2030; ByteDance/Tencent/Alibaba scramble for Ascend 950 chips after DeepSeek's V4 (BNN Bloomberg, Reuters).
- Q1 exports +14% y/y to $977.6B, but exports to the US fell 16% as SE Asia (+20%), EU (+21%) and Africa (+32%) surged (CNBC).

SINGAPORE
- Q1 2026 GDP grew 6.0% y/y, far above the 4.6% advance estimate, led by trade, manufacturing and finance on AI demand (Reuters).
- MTI kept its 2-4% full-year forecast but flagged energy-price risk from Hormuz; NODX +9.6% in Q1 on a 57.8% electronics jump (EnterpriseSG).
- Budget 2026 adds a 40% corporate tax rebate and a S$1.5B tranche to attract SGX listings; a new National AI Council will be chaired by PM Lawrence Wong (KPMG).

OUTLOOK: Records into the holiday, but Tuesday's reopen meets a live Hormuz energy shock — watch oil, the yuan's surge, and whether Middle East diplomacy holds.
