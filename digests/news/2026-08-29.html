DAILY NEWS DIGEST - 2026-08-29

INTERNATIONAL NEWS
- Nepal-Tibet flood catastrophe: an ice-rock avalanche on Tibet's Lhende River killed 470+ (some counts to 547) with 1,500+ still missing, including ~90 Americans; a rising barrier lake threatens further flooding.
- Iran is drafting conditions to reopen Strait of Hormuz shipping — demanding the US lift its port blockade, pay compensation, and remove sanctions; oil rose 2%+ after Trump threatened "economic warfare."
- The US official overseeing Trump's Gaza ceasefire publicly criticized Israel over continued attacks, warning the alternative is "the next war."

US STOCK MARKET
- Stocks fell Friday (Aug 28) after new Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole debut flagged persistent inflation: S&P 500 ~7,711 (-0.25%), Dow roughly flat, Nasdaq -0.30%.
- Nvidia gave back ~4.5%, surrendering about half of Thursday's ~8.4% post-earnings surge as rate fears hit AI names.
- Movers: Elastic +18% and 3M +3.7% on good news; IBM -2.4%, Home Depot -2.1%, Salesforce -1.6% lagged.

BIG TECH
- Nvidia's record Q2 (reported Aug 26) guided ~70% FY2028 revenue growth and $106-110B next quarter; server builders warned AI system prices will rise 15%+ on early-2027 Vera Rubin/Grace Blackwell shipments.
- Apple refreshed the Mac mini (first 2nm M6 chip, ~4x faster AI) and Mac Studio, and confirmed a Sept 9 event expected to unveil the iPhone 18 Pro and its first foldable.
- AWS committed to 2M Nvidia GPUs plus the new "Vera" CPU; Tesla shifted robotaxi focus to the wheel-free Cybercab with employee rides in Austin.

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS
- OpenAI quietly previewed "Astra," a multi-agent research system that solved 10 decade-old open math/CS problems; no release date.
- Google shipped Gemini 3.5 Flash to GA (new default in Search AI Mode) and launched Managed Agents in the Gemini API.
- Meta returned to open weights with Muse Code, Muse Spark 1.2, and the Apache-2.0 Muse Glimmer (29.6B); Z.AI released GLM-5.3 Flash.

OPENCLAW
- Steady incremental releases (late Aug): GPT-5.6 reasoning support, `openclaw attach` for coding agents, SQLite backup/restore, Puppeteer/CDP relay.
- Now 350k+ GitHub stars, 70k+ forks — one of the most-starred repos ever, ahead of React and Linux.
- Structural news remains earlier-2026: founder Peter Steinberger joined OpenAI; project moved to an independent open-source foundation.

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC
- A US federal judge ruled (Aug 28) the Pentagon's blacklisting of Anthropic as a "supply chain risk" unlawful First Amendment retaliation for the firm's criticism and its refusal to allow Claude in lethal-autonomous-weapons/mass surveillance.
- Anthropic signed a ~$45B compute deal with UK infra firm Nscale (Nvidia Vera Rubin, online late 2027).
- All Claude products now watermark generated text/files worldwide for EU AI Act compliance; Sonnet 5's $2/$10 pricing was made permanent.

CHINA
- July data softened: industrial output +4.5% y/y (missed), retail weak; H1 GDP +4.7% but Q2 eased to 4.3%.
- The US is preparing a 7.5% "overcapacity" tariff (restoring China duties to ~20%) ahead of a fall Xi-Trump summit in Washington; new chip tariffs deferred to June 2027 at an initial 0%.
- SMIC entered 5nm pilot runs for Huawei/Alibaba as Nvidia's China H200 sales stay stalled with no revenue booked.

SINGAPORE
- MTI raised 2026 GDP to 4.5-5.5% on the AI boom (Q2 +5.9% y/y); MAS unexpectedly tightened in late July on energy-driven inflation.
- National Day Rally: BTO income ceiling up to $16,000, a new SG Child Support Package replaces the Baby Bonus, and plans for a merged "western island" for industry.
- Sea Ltd reportedly overtook DBS as Singapore's largest company; Grab and Sea US shares launched on SGX via new depository receipts.

OUTLOOK: A hawkish Fed under Warsh cooled the Nvidia-led rally — AI capex is still the engine, but rate risk and the pre-summit China tariff are the near-term brakes.
