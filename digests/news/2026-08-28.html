DAILY NEWS DIGEST - 2026-08-28

INTERNATIONAL NEWS
- Himalaya glacier collapse triggered catastrophic flooding on the Nepal-Tibet border; 390+ confirmed dead and ~1,400 missing, including 500+ foreign nationals. USGS attributes the mag-5.2 seismic signal to the landslide itself.
- The official overseeing Trump's Gaza ceasefire plan publicly criticized Israel over strikes on the territory, warning the alternative is "the next war."
- Russia's Pacific Fleet tested Bastion missiles days after Putin visited disputed islands, prompting Tokyo to summon Russia's ambassador; Washington backed Japanese sovereignty.

US STOCK MARKET
- Nvidia earnings powered a rally (Aug 27 close): S&P 500 +0.72% to 7,730.99, Nasdaq +1.57% to 26,541.35, Dow +0.2% to 53,569.44 — best day since Aug 4.
- Nvidia jumped ~8-9% on $96.2B revenue (+106% YoY), data-center up 117%, guidance implying ~70% growth next year.
- Software surged on earnings: Okta +28%, Salesforce +22%, CrowdStrike +21%; Marvell fell 6% on weak margin guidance.

BIG TECH
- Nvidia reportedly nearing a ~$13B acquisition of Hugging Face, the open-source model hub (deal not yet signed).
- Alphabet lost ~$692B in market value on AI brain-drain fears as Gemini 3.5 Pro slips months behind schedule.
- Apple set its iPhone event for Sept 9 — first under new CEO John Ternus (takes over Sept 1); iPhone 18 Pro, a foldable, and Watch Series 12 expected.
- Meta agreed to pay up to $16.68B to settle claims it designed its apps to addict teens; Amazon to deploy 2M more Nvidia GPUs and is shutting Mechanical Turk after 21 years.

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS
- OpenAI claims its first custom inference chip beats Nvidia's Blackwell on AI work-per-watt in benchmarks (reported, not independently confirmed).
- Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 got an Aug 12 update — faster inference, scored 42/42 on the 2026 IMO, adds an "effort dial" for cost/capability tradeoffs.
- August model wave: DeepSeek-V4-Flash, GPT-5.6 "Luna" ($0.20/$1.20 per M tokens), Meta Muse Spark 1.1; Adobe+Johns Hopkins' "Wonder" turns an image into a walkable 3D world at 16 fps.

OPENCLAW
- OpenClaw (open-source self-hosted personal AI agent by Peter Steinberger) is now the most-starred GitHub repo at 346k+ stars, under 5 months after launch.
- Steinberger joined OpenAI, which is sponsoring the project; it stays open source under MIT.
- Latest releases add `openclaw attach` (temporary Claude Code/Codex access to a session), native subagents, and GPT-5.6 reasoning variants.

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC
- Claude in Chrome went GA on Aug 26; Claude also gained its own browser in "Cowork," with shared memory now spanning chat and Cowork.
- Claude Sonnet 5's intro pricing ($2/$10 per MTok) became permanent — the Sept 1 increase to $3/$15 was cancelled.
- Claude Code updates (v2.1.241, Aug 23) added prompt spellcheck, cost estimates, cloud sessions, remote control, and Bedrock/Alpine support.

CHINA
- H1 GDP grew 4.7% y/y (within the ~5% target), but Q2 eased to 4.3%; high-tech and services lead while property and big-ticket consumption stay weak.
- US weighing a new 7.5% "overcapacity" tariff (restoring China duties to ~20%) ahead of Xi's confirmed Sept 24 White House state visit.
- Nvidia added day-zero support for Alibaba's Qwen3, DeepSeek V4 Flash, and Z.ai's GLM 5.2 while warning against US curbs; Taiwan indicted an Nvidia manager over alleged B300 chip smuggling to China.

SINGAPORE
- MTI sharply upgraded 2026 GDP to 4.5-5.5% (from 2.0-4.0%) on the AI boom after Q2 grew 5.9% y/y; NODX forecast lifted to 14-16%.
- MAS unexpectedly tightened policy in late July on inflation risks tied to elevated energy costs.
- Sea Ltd posted 48% revenue growth to $7.79B in Q2; Grab raised full-year guidance and announced a $750M buyback.

OUTLOOK: Nvidia's blowout keeps the AI trade in charge, but Alphabet's $692B wipeout and the pre-Xi-summit tariff threat are reminders the rally is narrow and policy-exposed.
