BIG TECH

- The "Magnificent Seven" have staged a sharp recovery since the March 30 trough: Alphabet, Amazon, Nvidia, and Meta are each up 25%+ over four weeks.

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS
- Inception Labs released Mercury 2, a diffusion-based reasoning model that generates tokens in parallel at over 1,000 tokens/sec — a step-change for agentic loops and real-time voice AI.
- 2026 is shaping up as a consolidation year: key shifts include agentic workflow scaling, world models challenging LLM dominance, and hybrid architectures replacing pure transformers.
- Gartner projects 40% of enterprise applications will embed AI agents by mid-2026.

OPENCLAW
- OpenClaw's June 2026 release adds smarter conversational turn handling, more reliable model routing, safer session state, and stronger Codex CLI integration.
- Windows platform security support is now live: OpenClaw runs inside Microsoft Execution Containers (MXC) with a new Windows companion app for easy agent setup.

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC
- Anthropic launched Claude Tag (beta) on Slack — teams can @Claude directly in channels to delegate tasks, connect codebases, and query data. Anthropic says 65% of its own code is now generated by an internal version.
- Apple Foundation Models framework on iOS 27, iPadOS 27, and macOS 27 now supports Claude, extending native AI capabilities to Apple device users.

CHINA
- New trade secret regulations effective June 1, 2026 extend IP protection to data and algorithms, addressing AI and cloud computing — the first major update to China's 1995 trade secret framework.
- China's 15th Five-Year Plan targets AI, robotics, quantum computing, biomanufacturing, and 6G as strategic pillars, with indigenous R&D and supply chain self-sufficiency as the organizing principles.

SINGAPORE
- 58% of Singapore employers plan to freeze headcount in 2026, up from 50% in 2024, per the National Employers Federation survey, reflecting continued caution amid global uncertainty.
- Singapore now hosts 60+ AI Centres of Excellence, with PM Lawrence Wong chairing the National AI Council to align government, research, regulation, and industry on the country's AI agenda.

OUTLOOK: Markets digesting strong Big Tech earnings against mixed macro signals; AI infrastructure spending at record levels, but enterprise adoption and regulatory frameworks are beginning to catch up.
