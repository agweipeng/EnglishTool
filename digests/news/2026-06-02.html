DAILY NEWS DIGEST - 2026-06-02

INTERNATIONAL NEWS
- Iran peace talks hang by a thread: Iranian state media reported Tehran suspended negotiations after Israel struck Beirut's southern suburbs; Trump insisted talks continue "at rapid pace" and said Hezbollah agreed to halt attacks on Israel, but Iran's IRGC warned it could disrupt the Bab el-Mandeb Strait if Lebanon fighting continues. Brent crude briefly spiked to $97.44/bbl.
- Russia launched its heaviest attack on Kyiv in months overnight Tuesday, striking a 24-storey apartment building in the Podil district; at least four people injured with Zelenskyy having warned of an imminent massive strike the day prior.
- France and UK seized sanctioned Russian tanker Tagor in the Atlantic—the latest EU action against Russia's shadow fleet.

US STOCK MARKET
- All three indices closed at record highs June 1: DJIA +0.09% to 51,079, S&P 500 +0.26% to 7,600, NASDAQ +0.42% to 27,087—tech sector led (+2.5%) with NVIDIA surging 6% on Computex RTX Spark announcements.
- S&P 500 futures edged lower in early Asian trading June 2 as Iran ceasefire confusion revived geopolitical risk premium; WTI crude held gains after Monday's biggest daily rally in a month.
- Investors watch June 16-17 FOMC, PCE inflation data, and Hormuz oil supply developments as near-term catalysts.

BIG TECH
- NVIDIA dominated Computex Taipei: RTX Spark (ARM N1X, 1 petaflop, 128GB unified memory) arrives fall 2026 in PCs from Dell, HP, Microsoft Surface Laptop Ultra, ASUS, Lenovo, MSI; NVIDIA also launched Cosmos 3 world foundation model, Alpamayo 2 Super (32B, L4 robotaxi), and GR00T reference humanoid robot platform.
- Apple WWDC June 8 will debut Gemini-powered Siri: The Information reports Apple uses Google Gemini to distill on-device models and routes complex queries to Google Cloud with Nvidia Confidential Computing for privacy, all under Apple's "Private Cloud Compute" branding.
- Meta launched subscription tiers for Instagram, Facebook, and WhatsApp, bundling AI features as part of its accelerated monetization push.

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS
- MiniMax M3 launched (May 31): first open-weight model combining 1M-token context, frontier coding (59% SWE-Bench Pro, beating GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro), and native multimodality; new MiniMax Sparse Attention cuts per-token compute 20× at full context; weights to be published on Hugging Face within 10 days.
- JetBrains open-sourced Mellum2 (June 1): 12B MoE model (2.5B active parameters), Apache 2.0, built for routing, sub-agent tasks, and private deployment in software engineering pipelines.

OPENCLAW
- v2026.6.1-beta.1 released June 1: adds MiniMax M3 model support, Workboard orchestration primitives for multi-agent planning and run tracking, Skill Workshop governed proposal workflow, externalized @openclaw/tokenjuice and @openclaw/copilot plugins, and improved session recovery across interrupted tool calls and compaction handoffs.
- Project now at 376K GitHub stars.

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC
- Anthropic's "Dreaming" feature in Claude Managed Agents, detailed at Code with Claude London, lets agents write and share task notes so subsequent agents inherit context and avoid repeated mistakes—a persistent memory layer for multi-agent coding systems.
- Messages API update: system entries can now be injected inside the messages array mid-task, enabling real-time updates to permissions, token budgets, and environment context without breaking prompt cache.

CHINA
- Manufacturing PMI fell to 50.0 in May (from 50.3), a 3-month low; new orders slipped to 49.9 (contraction) while high-tech manufacturing held at 52.9. Domestic demand is the weak link—HSBC cut its 2026 retail sales forecast from 5.2% to 2.8%.
- New outbound investment rules (State Council, effective July 1): bans cross-border talent transfers in sensitive sectors without approval, gives Beijing power to force unwinding of completed overseas transactions, and allows retaliation against foreign firms from countries that restrict Chinese investment—explicitly targeting AI company "Singapore-washing."
- China's AI chip ecosystem is structurally diverging from the West: Huawei projected at 62% domestic market share; Ascend 950 outperforms Nvidia H20 by 50-150%; Nvidia's China share has effectively collapsed to zero.

SINGAPORE
- GovTech is building an AI Agent Registry (AI Assistant Desk) for 150,000 public officers, with customizable sanctioned-tool rules and security layers; planned wider rollout later in 2026—announced June 2.
- MTI held 2026 GDP forecast at 2-4%; Q1 GDP +6% YoY (six-quarter high); EnterpriseSG upgraded NODX forecast to 3-5% on AI-driven electronics demand. Iran war escalation remains the key downside risk.

OUTLOOK: US equities at all-time highs, but Iran talks fraying and a Russian Kyiv strike overnight add geopolitical weight—oil near $97/bbl and Hormuz still closed make energy cost the pivotal macro variable for H2 2026.
