LLM / AI APPLICATIONS

OPENCLAW

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC

CHINA

- January-July trade: general trade +10.2% YoY; Belt & Road trade +15.5%; private enterprise trade +17.2%; retail sales +2.6% YoY (services +5%, goods +1.1%).

SINGAPORE
