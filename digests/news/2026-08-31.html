DAILY NEWS DIGEST - 2026-08-31

INTERNATIONAL NEWS

US STOCK MARKET

BIG TECH

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS

- Model cadence stayed hot — 14 models across 8 providers in August (latest: Z.AI GLM-5.3 Flash).

OPENCLAW

- The headline stories (viral launch, ~346k stars, founder joining OpenAI, the 40k+ exposed-instances security crisis) all date to Jan-Feb 2026.

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC
- Anthropic opened a research preview of a Model Hardware Standard (MHS) letting Claude/agents safely operate robots and lab instruments, and hired a Google veteran to lead a new hardware effort.
- Claudeforce follow-on: Salesforce jumped ~22% (Agentforce ARR topped $1.5B, +240% YoY) and plans ~$300M of Anthropic token spend in 2026; analysts call it "the end of model-agnostic enterprise AI."
- On the Pentagon ruling, the government is expected to appeal; Claude Code CLI shipped a restricted/safe mode and a much smaller (~75MB) zstd Linux binary.

CHINA
- August official manufacturing PMI (due today) was seen at ~49.6 (Reuters poll), up from 49.2 but a second month in contraction; non-manufacturing was 49.0 in July.
- Q2 tech earnings diverged: Tencent beat (AI-marketing revenue +22% y/y, gaming +17%) while Alibaba missed badly ($1.26 EPS vs $1.85 expected).
- US is reviewing a loophole where Chinese firms access Nvidia GB300 chips via SE Asian data centers; key rare-earth and tariff deadlines cluster in November.

SINGAPORE
- July inflation picked up: headline CPI 2.2% (highest since Aug 2024), MAS core 2.0% y/y — after MAS's surprise late-July tightening; the 2026 GDP forecast holds at 4.5-5.5%.
- DBS Q2: NIM compressed to 1.87% but fee income rose 22% to S$1.7B (wealth +42%); total dividend 81 cents.
- Data-center buildout continues: ~1.4GW live capacity at ~2% vacancy, >S$30B hyperscaler commitments since 2024; Budget 2026 added S$800M for semiconductor R&D.

OUTLOOK: Record-August momentum meets a hawkish Warsh Fed and a fragile China truce — AI capex (Nvidia's $500B consortium, Anthropic's IPO) still leads, but rate and trade-deadline risk build into fall.
