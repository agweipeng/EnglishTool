BIG TECH

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS

OPENCLAW

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC

CHINA

- Post-Trump-Xi summit outlook: US-China trade war pause is expected to be extended, but expectations remain low for a structural deal; yuan strengthened to ~6.78 offshore on dollar weakness (Al Jazeera, Bloomberg).

SINGAPORE
- Q1 2026 GDP confirmed at 6.0% YoY (well above the 4.6% advance estimate), led by wholesale trade, manufacturing, and finance on AI demand; NODX +9.6% with electronics +57.8% (Reuters, Yahoo Finance).
- Singapore launches a National AI Council chaired by PM Lawrence Wong, integrating AI across manufacturing, finance, and healthcare; the city-state is also first in Asia to mandate Sustainable Aviation Fuel blending in 2026 (KPMG, Budget 2026).
- Carbon tax set at S$45/tonne (highest in Asia); MTI maintains 2-4% full-year GDP growth forecast but flags Hormuz energy-price risk as the key downside (MTI, EnterpriseSG).

OUTLOOK: Markets face a tug-of-war between record tech earnings momentum (Micron, Nvidia) and live Hormuz/Iran energy risk — oil, yuan, and Middle East diplomatic signals are the tape-watchers' focus for Wednesday.
