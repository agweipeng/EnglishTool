DAILY NEWS DIGEST - 2026-08-20

INTERNATIONAL NEWS
- Mount Etna erupted in Sicily, sending smoke and ash skyward; occurred a day after a US tourist died from a lightning strike while hiking the volcano.

- India's BJP government is navigating a "cockroach movement" — youth-led protests after an official compared unemployed graduates to cockroaches, amplifying an ongoing education and jobs crisis.

US STOCK MARKET
- S&P 500 snapped a three-day losing streak on Aug 19 after the Treasury Department announced plans to more than double buybacks of 10-, 20-, and 30-year debt to ease long-end bond market pressure.
- Aug 20 futures: S&P 500 +0.1%, Nasdaq-100 +0.3%, Dow +0.04% — cautious optimism as bond intervention holds.

BIG TECH
- Microsoft logged its best trading day since 2008 on earnings beat + raised capex guidance; Alphabet added $400B+ in market cap after strong cloud growth; both committed to EU AI watermarking code.
- Apple issued a weaker-than-expected Q3 revenue forecast citing supply constraints and forthcoming price increases; also overhauled Europe App Store fees to resolve a payments compliance clash.
- Meta released Glimmer, an open-weight AI model anyone can run locally, alongside the API-only Muse Spark — a dual-track strategy targeting both open and enterprise markets.

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS
- OpenAI launched "Ultrafast" mode for GPT-5.6 Sol on Aug 13: 14× speed increase; the model family now spans Sol (workhorse), Terra (intermediate), and Luna (budget).
- Google DeepMind released a new real-time interactive general-purpose world model in August.
- Zhihu's trending data confirms DeepSeek repriced its API, triggering users to reassess Gemini Flash 3.7 — which has regained traction as a strong mid-tier option.
- Anthropic announced text watermarking for Claude-generated content, with Microsoft and Google also signing the EU AI watermarking code.

OPENCLAW
- OpenClaw 2026.8.1 broad release ships atomic model/runtime switching, SQLite snapshot backup/restore, and stronger secret egress controls — the release focuses on production-grade hardening.
- Founder Peter Steinberger joined OpenAI in February 2026; the project continues under a community foundation with OpenAI's commitment to keep it open-source.

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC
- Claude Sonnet 5 is now the default model for Pro, Team Standard, and Enterprise tiers; native 1M-token context window and adaptive thinking are on by default.
- August updates: self-hosted environments, cross-session messaging, removed subagent spawn cap, new Focus view in VSCode, and improved sandbox credential masking.

CHINA
- July exports +23.9% YoY (beat 22.2% forecast); imports +27.5%; trade surplus $112.5B (vs $107B estimate), supported by global AI infrastructure demand.
- China-EU trade tensions are escalating, with Beijing publishing a policy document showing little room for compromise; retail sales remain nearly flat despite strong headline trade numbers.

SINGAPORE
- GDP growth forecast raised to 4.5–5.5% for 2026 (from 2–4% in February); Q2 GDP +5.7% YoY, goods sector up 10.4%.
- PM Lawrence Wong announced AI productivity programs targeting worker concerns about job displacement; JPMorgan raised its Singapore equities target citing robust growth and narrowing valuation gaps.

OUTLOOK: Bond market stabilization and strong Big Tech earnings are holding equities up; AI infrastructure capex cycle remains the dominant macro driver across markets.
