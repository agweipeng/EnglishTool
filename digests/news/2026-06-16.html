DAILY NEWS DIGEST - 2026-06-16

INTERNATIONAL NEWS
- US and Iran reached a preliminary peace agreement reopening the Strait of Hormuz, sharply easing global energy supply fears and sending oil prices lower.
- SpaceX is advancing toward an IPO at a valuation that would make Elon Musk history's first trillionaire; market observers are flagging bubble risk.
- UK enacted a social media ban for users under 16, intensifying global debate over digital regulation and state oversight of tech platforms.

US STOCK MARKET
- S&P 500 +1.7%, Dow +469 pts to a record close, Nasdaq +3.1% — best single session since March 31 — driven by the US-Iran deal easing inflation and energy fears.
- Tech, communication services, and consumer discretionary led; seven of 11 S&P sectors finished higher.

BIG TECH
- Combined AI capex from Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, and Meta is projected at $649B in 2026, up 58% from $411B in 2025; the AI infra arms race accelerates.
- Nvidia holds its position as world's most valuable company at $4T+; the Magnificent Seven represents ~33% of S&P 500 performance weight.

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS
- Anthropic released Claude Fable 5 (Mythos-class), its most capable publicly available model, with safety guardrails for high-risk domains; accessible via Claude Code v2.1.170+.
- OpenAI is preparing a confidential IPO filing targeting September 2026 at a $730B private valuation. NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B also launched June 4.

OPENCLAW
- Microsoft launched Scout, a persistent AI assistant built on the OpenClaw framework, integrated into Microsoft 365 with adaptive user-created skills and built-in safety audits.
- June security update (2026.6.x) patched phishing vulnerabilities, tightened Docker sandbox, and added OpenRouter as first-class onboarding flow. OpenClaw now at 310K+ GitHub stars.

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC
- Billing change effective June 15: Agent SDK and claude -p usage now billed at API list prices separately from subscription limits — affects all programmatic Claude users.
- Claude Code gained nested sub-agents, smarter model/region handling, plugin search, and improved Chrome, VSCode, and terminal workflows in recent releases.

CHINA
- Commerce Minister Wang Wentao heads to Europe to advance the China-EU Trade and Investment Consultation Mechanism amid ongoing trade frictions.
- The 15th Five-Year Plan designates AI, robotics, quantum computing, biomanufacturing, and 6G as strategic future industries; tech fixed asset investment up 20%+ YoY.

SINGAPORE
- Singapore's National AI Council (chaired by the Prime Minister) is directing a national AI transformation agenda covering four key industry sectors under Budget 2026.
- Crypto stablecoin framework Phase 2 (Jan–Jun 2026) active with stricter asset-backed reserve requirements; Phase 3 opens algorithmic stablecoins from July.

OUTLOOK: US-Iran deal flips the risk-off switch; Nasdaq +3.1% sets bullish tone for AI earnings season, but $649B combined Big Tech capex sets a demanding bar for Q2 results.
