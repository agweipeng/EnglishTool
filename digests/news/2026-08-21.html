DAILY NEWS DIGEST - 2026-08-21

INTERNATIONAL NEWS
- US VP Vance declared the Iran conflict has entered a "new phase" of economic pressure; Treasury Secretary Bessent told CNBC this will be "the greatest coordinated economic isolation in history," with Iran facing water shortages and power outages alongside ongoing executions of detained protesters.
- Ukraine is critically short on interceptor missiles amid continued Russian strikes; separately, Russian authorities arrested 8 people for planning an attack on a strategic defense facility in the Moscow region.

US STOCK MARKET
- Markets rebounded Aug 21: S&P 500 +0.42%, Dow +0.51%, Nasdaq +0.46%, recovering from a sharp Aug 20 sell-off (Dow -1.3%, S&P -0.8%).
- Bitcoin surged alongside Iran sanctions news; Russell 2000 lagged at -1.34%, signaling the rally is concentrated in large-cap/risk-on names rather than broad.

BIG TECH
- Apple fell ~9% post-earnings, erasing $350B+ in market cap on memory supply constraints and a weak Q3 revenue outlook; Meta lost ~$85B in market cap on its earnings report.
- Tesla went cash flow negative and guided for higher spending, shedding ~$7B in market value; contrasted with Microsoft (+15% post-earnings) and Nvidia (+85% YoY revenue to $81.6B).
- Combined 2026 capex for Amazon, Google, Meta, and Microsoft is tracking ~$740B — up 77% from $410B in 2025.

LLM / AI APPLICATIONS
- Anthropic's Claude Mythos 5 ranks #1 overall on BenchLM with a score of 82.95/100; 128 AI models have been released in the past 12 months — roughly one every 3 days.
- GLM-5.2 Turbo (Z.AI) released Aug 17; 11 of the top 50 BenchLM models (22%) are now open-weight, reflecting accelerating open-source parity.
- Million-token context windows are now standard across flagship models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and xAI.

OPENCLAW
- OpenClaw 2026.8.1 now operates under a nonprofit foundation steward (MIT-licensed, $0 pricing), adding atomic model/runtime switching, SQLite snapshot backup/restore, macOS app profile isolation, and shared plugin lifecycle monitors.

CLAUDE CODE / ANTHROPIC
- Claude Code Aug 21 update: new prompt and runner settings, tighter MCP and plugin security, more resilient Remote Control and cross-session messaging, startup speed improvements, and fixes for memory leaks and terminal glitches.
- Anthropic launched Claude Academy — a learning hub with courses, tutorials, and badges focused on safe and effective AI use with Claude.

CHINA
- PBOC and SAFE expanded cross-border RMB and foreign currency operations for multinationals (announced Aug 17); M2 money supply +7.7% YoY, M1 +4%.
- High-tech manufacturing output +13.8% YoY (Jan-Jul): 3D printing +52.3%, lithium-ion batteries +40.2%, industrial robots +28.5%.

SINGAPORE
- The Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone is deepening cross-border integration across manufacturing, logistics, digital economy, and business services.
- Singapore's S$37B RIE 2030 research masterplan covers advanced manufacturing, semiconductors, biomedical science, sustainability tech, and quantum computing; 60+ AI Centres of Excellence now operational.

OUTLOOK: Markets rebounding on Iran geopolitics and Bitcoin momentum, but the Big Tech earnings divergence — Microsoft soaring, Apple and Meta falling — signals investors are rewarding AI execution over scale alone.
